# Umpire Levels — Fall Indoor League 2026

Internal notes for randomizing umpire assignments. **Not shown on the dashboard.**

## Level rules

Certification is checked first, then total matches umpired
(Summer 2026 + Fall 2025 + Winter 2026 + duties done in this league).

| Level | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Senior** | Certified and 10+ matches |
| **Intermediate** | Certified with under 10 matches (including 0), or not certified with 10+ |
| **Developing** | Not certified, 1–9 matches |
| **New** | Not certified, never umpired |

- An umpire who is not certified can reach Intermediate but not Senior.
- Levels are re-checked as duties in this league are completed: add each duty to the
  "This League" count and move the umpire up when they cross a threshold.

## Sources

- **Summer 2026:** T20 + F40 matches; this is also the only sheet with certification status.
  Anyone not on it is treated as not certified.
- **Fall 2025** and **Winter 2026:** total matches umpired.

## How levels will be used when randomizing

1. An umpire never covers a match involving their own team, or the slot right before or after it.
2. Umpires are only placed in slots they are available for.
3. Every match has at least one Senior or Intermediate umpire — never two New umpires together.
4. The semi-finals and the Final get Senior umpires first.
5. Duties are spread evenly so nobody is overloaded (especially one-umpire teams).
6. Among equally suitable umpires, the pick is random.
7. Any slot that cannot meet these rules is flagged for the organizer instead of being filled anyway.
